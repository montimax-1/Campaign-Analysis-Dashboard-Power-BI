## 📌 Project Overview

A multi-page interactive marketing analytics dashboard built in **Power BI** to analyze the performance of **1,000+ campaigns** across **8 marketing channels** — helping stakeholders track revenue, ROI, customer engagement, and conversion efficiency at a glance.

## 🗂️ Dashboard Pages

### 1. Campaign Overview
- Revenue breakdown by channel (pie chart)
- ROI comparison across all 8 channels (bar chart)
- Conversion trend over time (line chart)
- Campaign-to-channel mapping table
- Filters: Region (East/North/South/West), Age Group (18–60+)

### 2. Channel Performance
- Channel-wise revenue and spend comparison
- Spend distribution by channel (donut chart)
- ROI% by channel (bar chart)
- Monthly revenue trend by channel (multi-line chart)
- Summary table: Total Spend, Revenue, ROI%, Avg. CTR per channel

### 3. Customer Engagement
- Engagement by campaign (bar chart — 45+ campaigns)
- Total engagement score by day (line chart)
- Total clicks by campaign type (donut chart)
- KPIs: Avg. CTR (49.27%), Avg. Engagement (54.27%)

### 4. Conversion & ROI
- Revenue vs. Spend by campaign (grouped bar chart)
- Top campaigns by ROI% (horizontal bar chart)
- Conversion trends over time (line chart)
- Channel type filter: Digital vs. Offline

### 5. Executive Summary
- Monthly revenue vs. spend trend
- Total conversions by channel
- Spend distribution by channel
- Top campaigns by revenue table

## 🔍 Key Insights from the Dashboard

- **Website** and **Branch** are the top revenue-generating channels (~$6.5M each)
- **Instagram** has the lowest ROI% (12.16%) and highest room for optimization
- **SMS** drives the most clicks (14.59% share) despite lower spend
- Conversion dips sharply around **Month 4** — potential seasonal or campaign fatigue issue
- All channels maintain a near-identical **Avg. CTR of 49.27%**, suggesting uniform audience quality
